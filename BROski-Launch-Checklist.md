## 5. Market Health — Build an Honest, Usable Market

### Why
A huge gap between bids and asks makes the token feel abandoned. Small, genuine activity is more valuable than artificial hype.

### Before touching the market
- [ ] Set a personal MINTME budget that is genuinely affordable to lose.
- [ ] Record the baseline: last trade, bid/ask spread, buy depth, sell depth, number of active orders, and 7-day volume.
- [ ] Write the goal in plain English: “Enable small voluntary trades and price discovery,” not “pump the price.”
- [ ] Tell participants that crypto assets are high-risk and they must make their own decisions.

### Order-book actions
- [ ] Cancel or review obviously unrealistic / stale orders that belong to your account.
- [ ] Place a small number of transparent, realistic buy orders at staggered levels — only with your own budget.
- [ ] Place a small number of realistic sell orders at staggered levels; avoid a giant intimidating sell wall.
- [ ] Avoid trading with your own accounts to create fake volume (wash trading).
- [ ] Encourage willing community members to trade only if they understand the risks and want the utility.
- [ ] Review the order book weekly; change strategy based on genuine demand, not emotion.

### Dashboard metrics
- [ ] First 3 genuine trades.
- [ ] First 10 genuine trades.
- [ ] Bid/ask spread reduced to a sensible, visible range.
- [ ] At least 3 independent active market participants.
- [ ] 30-day volume tracked alongside holder activity and community retention.

🏆 **WIN:** The market has real voluntary participation, clear risk messaging, and no fake activity.
